One image summing up Monday 23 November 2026 (1024×1024): COP31 collapsing with no deal in Antalya, Thanksgiving storms battering both US coasts, Delta's automation outage delaying 1,600 flights, the Houthis claiming a strike on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery, the Russian cargo ship Sparta II still burning off Finland, and a drone strike trapping 14 in the Kyiv region.

Each of the six stories was checked against reporting from Reuters, CNN, UN News, the Wall Street Journal and ISW. The illustrations in `assets/` were generated for this entry and carry no lettering; all words are drawn by `draw.py` with DejaVu, over a weather bar of real Open-Meteo forecast figures for five of the story cities. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) hands the finished `entry.png` to the grader byte-for-byte.

## Reproduce it

```sh
python3 draw.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
